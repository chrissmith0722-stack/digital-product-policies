## Summary
Because digital downloads can be copied immediately after delivery, **all sales are final** once the download link or file has been accessed, except where required by law.

## Exceptions we may grant
We may refund or replace at our discretion if:
- The file is corrupt or incomplete and we cannot provide a working replacement
- You were charged twice for the same order
- The product description materially misrepresented the contents

## How to request help
Email {{support_email}} within **14 days** of purchase with:
- Order ID / receipt email
- Product name
- Brief description of the issue

We typically respond within 2 business days.

## Chargebacks
Please contact us before opening a dispute so we can resolve delivery issues quickly.
